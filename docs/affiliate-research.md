## Decision method

Evaluate product fit, seller conversion and delivered customer cost alongside commission. For a controlled comparison use:

`estimated earnings per 100 outbound clicks = 100 × conversion rate × commissionable order value × effective commission rate × approval rate`

Example only: 2% conversion × £80 commissionable value × 6% commission × 90% approved sales gives £8.64 per 100 clicks. These are invented inputs illustrating the calculation, not merchant results. Attribution and conversion must be measured, not assumed.
